Undergraduate Thesis · KUET · 2026Thesis completed · manuscript submitted

Open-Source RFIC Design & Verification of a 28 GHz Two-Stage Class-A SiGe Power Amplifier

Designed a two-stage Class-A power amplifier centered at 28 GHz in the IHP SG13G2 130 nm SiGe BiCMOS Open PDK and developed a progressive path from schematic-level RF design toward more physically representative process- and EM-aware realization.

  • Built and verified the RF circuit using Qucs-S with ngspice/Xyce-based simulation and Python-assisted analysis.
  • Studied the transition from ideal passives to geometry-controlled PDK components and EM-characterized passive structures using KLayout and openEMS-compatible workflows.
  • Used machine learning as a supporting design-space exploration tool while keeping circuit simulation and RF verification as the primary evidence.
  • The thesis-derived physical-realization manuscript has been submitted to ICCIT 2026.
