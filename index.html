Open-Source RFIC Design & Verification of a 28 GHz Two-Stage Class-A SiGe Power Amplifier

Developed an open-source-compatible 28 GHz SiGe RFIC workflow spanning circuit design, process-aware realization, data-assisted design exploration, and independent simulation-based verification.

Independent Research · 2026–PresentOngoing

GNN-SABLE: GNN-Guided VLSI Floorplanning with Constraint-Aware Refinement

Investigating how learning-derived structural guidance can support constraint-rich VLSI floorplanning while keeping legality and physical-design requirements explicit.

  • Studies the interaction between learned guidance and classical physical-design optimization.
  • Emphasizes legal, compact, and constraint-aware layouts under controlled evaluation.
  • Uses public floorplanning benchmarks for reproducible experimentation without exposing the unpublished implementation details.

Leakage-Aware RTL-to-QoR Prediction for Early Area & Timing Estimation

Working with Zakia Sultana, Ph.D. Candidate in the Electronic Design Automation Group at the MSCAD Laboratory, University of Arkansas, on early implementation-quality estimation directly from RTL.

  • Studies whether structural information available before mapping can provide useful early area and timing insight.

  • Explores explainability and reliability awareness so predictions can be interpreted with appropriate engineering caution.

Selected Engineering Projects

Earlier engineering projects that complement my current RFIC, VLSI/EDA, and hardware-focused research.

RF / Microwave

Dual-Band Microstrip Patch Antenna

Designed and simulated a dual-band microstrip patch antenna operating near 10 GHz and 21 GHz using CST Studio Suite, with analysis of impedance matching, VSWR, radiation behavior, and surface-current distribution.

Digital Signal Processing

FIR Filter Design Using Windowing Techniques

Implemented and analyzed low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, and band-stop FIR filters in MATLAB using rectangular, Hann, Hamming, and Blackman windows, including frequency-response and audio-filtering experiments.
