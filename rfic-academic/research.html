Undergraduate Thesis · KUET · 2026

Open-Source RFIC Design and Verification of a 28-GHz Two-Stage Class-A SiGe Power Amplifier with Machine-Learning-Assisted Optimization

Research question: How can an open-source-compatible RFIC flow carry a mmWave power amplifier from schematic design toward process-aware and EM-aware realization while preserving gain, matching and stability?
  • Designed and simulated a two-stage 28-GHz Class-A SiGe power amplifier using the IHP SG13G2 130-nm SiGe BiCMOS Open PDK and an open-source RFIC workflow based on Qucs-S, ngspice/Xyce, KLayout and EM-aware modeling.
  • Developed a progressive ideal-passive → PDK-aware → EM-aware realization workflow to study how increasingly realistic passive implementation changes RF performance.
  • The final process-aware configuration achieved approximately 17.4 dB gain at 28 GHz, with S11/S22 near −13 dB and K ≈ 12.
  • Used machine learning as a supporting design-exploration layer for candidate screening while retaining circuit simulation as the primary verification authority.
  • Currently extending the work toward layout/GDSII-level realization; a first-author thesis-derived manuscript has been submitted to ICCIT 2026.

RF Matching & Microwave Design

Published · IEEE MARSCON 2026

LMatchNet-V: Vendor-Aware RF L-Section Matching

  • Coauthored a physics-seeded framework that starts from analytical L-section synthesis and learns residual corrections to localize a compact vendor-component search region.
  • Final inductor-capacitor pairs are selected through frequency-dependent vendor-model recomputation rather than direct ML prediction, keeping circuit evaluation as the final decision authority.
  • Adaptive local safe-library search reduces the number of candidate pairs that must be evaluated while retaining strong recovery of vendor-feasible matching solutions.

RF / Microwave Project

Dual-Band Microstrip Patch Antenna

  • Designed and simulated a dual-band microstrip patch antenna near 10 GHz and 21 GHz in CST Studio Suite, tuning the structure for the targeted resonant bands.
  • Evaluated impedance matching, S-parameters, VSWR, radiation behavior and surface-current distributions to understand the antenna response at both operating frequencies.
  • The project strengthened practical experience in microwave structure design, full-wave EM simulation and interpretation of frequency-domain antenna performance.

Complementary VLSI/EDA Work

Manuscript SubmittedFirst Author

Learning-Guided VLSI Floorplanning

  • Developing learning-guided physical-design methods for constraint-rich, outline-aware VLSI floorplanning using graph-based circuit representations.
  • Combining learned guidance with B*-tree simulated annealing, structured legalization and exact feasibility checks so that ML improves the search while explicit optimization preserves hard physical constraints.
  • Investigating the neural-legality gap: learned models can capture strong global placement geometry, but exact local non-overlap and discrete feasibility still require structured optimization.
  • The broader goal is to understand where learning should guide physical-design search and where combinatorial reasoning remains necessary.

Ongoing Research

Leakage-Aware RTL-to-QoR Prediction

  • Collaborating on early prediction of mapped area and pre-layout timing directly from RTL, before full mapping and timing-analysis workflows are complete.
  • Building source-aware evaluation around provenance tracking, duplicate and near-duplicate control and component-aware grouping to reduce leakage and better measure generalization to genuinely different RTL designs.
  • Studying structural-complexity regimes, size-aware prediction, explainability and coverage awareness using RTL-derived features with Yosys and OpenSTA-based flows.
  • The research aims not only to predict QoR early, but also to identify when a prediction is trustworthy and which RTL structures are driving it.
