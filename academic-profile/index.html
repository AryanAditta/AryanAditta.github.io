Selected research

Undergraduate Thesis · KUET · 2026

Open-Source RFIC Design and Verification of a 28 GHz Two-Stage Class-A SiGe Power Amplifier

Designed and simulated a 28 GHz Class-A SiGe power amplifier using the IHP SG13G2 Open PDK and open-source-compatible RFIC tools; progressed the work toward PDK/EM-aware realization, ML-assisted design exploration, physical layout, and publication.

Research Collaboration · Ongoing

Leakage-Aware RTL-to-QoR Prediction

Working on pre-mapping area and timing prediction from RTL with emphasis on source provenance, leakage-aware evaluation, structural feature engineering, explainability, and reproducible machine-learning workflows.

Independent Research · 2026–Present

GNN-SABLE: Learning-Guided VLSI Floorplanning

Investigating learning-guided VLSI floorplanning under physical-design constraints, with controlled evaluation and reproducible experimentation for future publication.
