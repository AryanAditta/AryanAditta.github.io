VLSI/EDA · Learning-Guided Physical Design · RTL-to-QoR Prediction

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering graduate from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET). My research interests center on VLSI design automation, physical design and machine learning methods that provide useful guidance without obscuring the underlying engineering constraints.

My current work spans constraint-rich floorplanning and early RTL-level QoR prediction. Across both directions, I am interested in where learned representations are genuinely useful, where explicit algorithmic reasoning remains necessary and how evaluation can stay reproducible and leakage-aware.

Featured Research Direction

Learning-Guided Constraint-Rich VLSI Floorplanning

I study how learned circuit representations can guide floorplanning while exact geometric legality and physical-design constraints remain explicit. My submitted GNN-SABLE work combines learned graph guidance with structured optimization, while ongoing work examines the broader gap between strong global neural geometry and exact local feasibility.
