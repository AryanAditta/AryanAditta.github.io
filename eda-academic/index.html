VLSI/EDA · Physical Design · RTL Area & Timing Prediction

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering graduate from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET). My research focuses on VLSI design automation where learning can guide difficult decisions while geometry, feasibility and physical evaluation remain explicit.

My current work spans GRACE, a GNN-guided relational floorplanning framework, and source-aware RTL area/timing prediction. I am also extending the RTL-QoR workflow toward reproducible pre-layout power labels and stage-wise OpenROAD physical-design outcomes while keeping those extension pilots separate from the currently validated Area/Timing results.
