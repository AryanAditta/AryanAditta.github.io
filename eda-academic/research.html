Physical Design Research · 2026–Present

GRACE: GNN-Guided Relational Decoding with Convex Shaping and Exact Repair

Research question: Can a learned relational representation provide useful global floorplan structure while deterministic decoding, convex optimization and verified repair enforce legality and expose how much of the final gain actually comes from learning?
S1 · GNN relational decodingPredict separation relations, order keys and soft-block aspect ratios, then decode a hard-legal start.
S2 · Convex shapingResize and place blocks for the decoded topology before grid snapping.
S3 · Exact repairCompact and packed routes combine constraint surgery, local moves and CP-SAT windows.
S4 · Leakage-free portfolioRetain the route with lower surrogate cost without reading the reference layout.
GRACE pipeline · submitted to ICEEICT 2027
  • The GNN predicts relations rather than final coordinates: pairwise separation structure, ordering keys and soft-block shapes are converted by a deterministic decoder into a hard-legal starting layout.
  • A four-stage pipeline combines relational decoding, convex shaping, exact repair along compact and packed routes and leakage-free portfolio selection. Under the paper's legality definition, every reported stage remains hard-legal.
  • On 100 unseen FloorSet-Lite confirmation layouts, GRACE achieved mean evaluation cost 1.501 versus 7.353 for matched-time single-worker PARSAC CA-SA, with HPWL/GT 1.177 versus 1.895 at essentially equal mean runtime (56.6 s versus 56.7 s).
  • A controlled start-stage ablation separates learning from post-processing: replacing the learned start with random relations preserves much of the cost advantage after repair but loses about 85% of the wirelength advantage, showing that the learned component primarily improves global relational structure.
  • Boundary, grouping and MIB constraints remain soft-scored in this formulation, so the work does not claim universal fixed-outline or all-constraint satisfaction; remaining MIB and compactness limitations are reported explicitly.

RTL-to-QoR Research · 2026–Present

Source-Aware Evaluation of RTL Area and Timing Prediction with Prospective Confirmation

Research question: Can early RTL area and timing predictors be evaluated without source leakage while preserving information chronology and confirming improvements on genuinely fresh physical labels?
Open full research overview ↗
  • The current journal draft evaluates mapped standard-cell area and standardized pre-layout timing under a pinned Yosys/ABC, OpenSTA and Nangate45 flow.
  • Its development corpus contains 681 labelled configurations grouped into 498 source components, with source-connected grouping and model-specific prediction freezes used to preserve information chronology.
  • On fresh prospective cohorts, the Area system reduced MAE from 8,382 to 3,015 µm² on 39 paired designs, while TimingV2 reduced MAE from 0.167 to 0.136 ns on a separate 39-design paired timing cohort. The manuscript keeps the stronger Area result and the more metric-sensitive Timing result distinct.
  • Power extension in progress: a reusable RTL-to-power pilot is being developed around a fixed technology/library and activity contract, separating leakage, internal, switching and total power and optionally validating a smaller subset with simulation-derived activity.
  • Physical-design extension in progress: a separate OpenROAD pilot carries selected RTL designs from synthesis and pre-layout OpenSTA through floorplanning, placement, CTS and routing while preserving stage-wise timing, area, utilization, wirelength, congestion and physical artifacts.
  • These Power and OpenROAD efforts are currently feasibility and labeling-pipeline extensions; they are not yet presented as completed prediction results or as evidence already integrated into the journal manuscript.
