Physical Design Research · 2026–Present

Learning-Guided Constraint-Rich VLSI Floorplanning

Research question: How much of high-quality constrained floorplanning can be learned from circuit structure and where is explicit combinatorial optimization still necessary?

I investigate graph-based guidance for floorplanning under connectivity, geometry and placement constraints while exact legality remains the responsibility of structured optimization.

  • The submitted GNN-SABLE work combines graph-based guidance with B*-tree annealing, structured legalization and exact feasibility handling.
  • Ongoing controlled studies examine the neural-legality gap and separate representation quality from output/feasibility limitations.

Preparing for Integration, the VLSI Journal · Public Reproducibility Package

Source-Aware Evaluation of RTL Area and Timing Prediction with Prospective Confirmation

Research question: Can early RTL area and timing predictors be evaluated in a way that prevents source leakage, preserves information chronology and confirms improvements on labels that did not exist when the predictions were frozen?
  • Evaluates mapped standard-cell area and standardized pre-layout minimum feasible clock period under one pinned open-source Yosys/ABC, OpenSTA and Nangate45 flow.
  • The development corpus contains 681 labelled configurations grouped into 498 source-connected components so related RTL sources do not cross evaluation folds.
  • On 39 paired fresh Area designs, the frozen Area system reduced MAE from 8,382 to 3,015 µm² and the primary macro log loss by 58.9% relative to its frozen native reference.
  • On 39 paired fresh Timing designs, TimingV2 reduced MAE from 0.167 to 0.136 ns and the primary loss by 17.5%; the manuscript explicitly reports that this timing gain is more metric-sensitive than the Area result.
  • The public package releases frozen predictions, labels, protocol records, result tables and scripts while preserving the stated limits on arbitrary-RTL support, post-layout/signoff accuracy and cross-technology transfer.

Broader ML Evaluation Research

Enhancer Benchmark Audit & Model Evaluation

Submitted to BMC BioinformaticsFirst Author

Benchmark audit and model evaluation for enhancer prediction: data overlap, training adequacy and assay transfer

This study focuses on evaluation validity rather than proposing a new prediction architecture. It audits benchmark independence, re-evaluates enhancer classification with similarity grouping and composition matching, and separately studies quantitative lentiMPRA activity prediction and external assay transfer.

  • Identified 100 strong historical test records identical to training records in supplied copies of both legacy enhancer-classification tasks.
  • Separates single-seed and ensemble conclusions for DNABERT/DNABERT-2 and tests training-duration effects rather than treating architecture alone as the explanation.
  • Evaluates DNABERT-2, LegNet and MPRAnn on measured reporter activity and shows that positive external ranking can coexist with poor absolute calibration under assay shift.
