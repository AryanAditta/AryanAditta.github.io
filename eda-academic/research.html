Physical Design Research · 2026–Present

Learning-Guided Constraint-Rich VLSI Floorplanning

I investigate how graph-based learning can guide floorplanning under connectivity, geometric and placement constraints while exact legality remains the responsibility of structured optimization.

  • The submitted GNN-SABLE work studies learned guidance for B*-tree-based floorplanning and how a learned global prior can assist a classical optimization process.
  • Ongoing experiments examine the neural-legality gap: models can recover useful global geometry while exact local packing remains substantially harder.
  • Current controlled studies separate encoder quality from output representation to identify where the remaining feasibility limitation actually lies.

Research Collaboration · MSCAD Laboratory, University of Arkansas

Leakage-Aware RTL-to-QoR Prediction

I am working with Zakia Sultana, Ph.D. Candidate at the MSCAD Laboratory, University of Arkansas, on early estimation of implementation quality directly from RTL before full technology mapping and static timing analysis.

  • The work studies how structural information available at RTL can support early area and timing prediction.
  • The data pipeline emphasizes source provenance, duplicate-aware grouping and leakage-aware evaluation so model performance reflects genuinely unseen source families.
  • Current research examines structural-complexity effects, explainability and coverage awareness so estimates can be interpreted with appropriate engineering caution.
