Physical Design Research · 2026–Present

Learning-Guided Constraint-Rich VLSI Floorplanning

Research question: How much of high-quality constrained floorplanning can be learned from circuit structure and where is explicit combinatorial optimization still necessary?
Open full research overview ↗
  • Investigates graph-based guidance for floorplanning while exact geometry and hard legality remain explicit rather than delegated to the learned model.
  • The submitted GNN-SABLE work combines graph-based guidance with B*-tree simulated annealing, structured legalization and exact feasibility handling.
  • Controlled follow-up studies examine the neural-legality gap and separate representation quality from output and feasibility limitations.
  • The work is positioned as learning-guided optimization rather than end-to-end replacement of the physical-design search process.

RTL-to-QoR Research · 2026–Present

Source-Aware Evaluation of RTL Area and Timing Prediction with Prospective Confirmation

Research question: Can early RTL area and timing predictors be evaluated without source leakage while preserving information chronology and confirming improvements on genuinely fresh physical labels?
Open full research overview ↗
  • Evaluates mapped standard-cell area and standardized pre-layout timing under a pinned Yosys/ABC, OpenSTA and Nangate45 flow.
  • Uses source-connected grouping so related RTL designs do not cross development and evaluation folds.
  • Freezes candidate models and predictions before generating fresh labels, making prospective confirmation part of the evaluation protocol rather than a retrospective split.
  • Current results show a strong fresh-area confirmation and a smaller, more metric-sensitive timing improvement; the manuscript keeps those distinctions explicit.
