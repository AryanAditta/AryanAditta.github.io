Privacy & Analytics

Optional website analytics

This academic website uses Google Analytics only if you choose “Allow analytics”. Declining keeps analytics disabled. You can change your choice using “Analytics choices” in the footer.

Analytics uses browser cookies to estimate distinct browsers, visits, page views, engagement, returning visits, approximate country and referral sources. It also measures clicks on the CV, research and publications pages, research overview images and professional profiles. Email campaign links contain general campaign labels, never recipient names or email addresses.

These statistics cannot reliably identify a particular professor or person. Ad blockers, declined consent and private browsing can reduce counts or count one person more than once. CV clicks measure opening a link, not proof that a document was read.

Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in the website implementation. No email-open pixels, fingerprinting or recipient identifiers are used. We do not separately log IP addresses.

Google processes analytics information according to How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Learn more in Google’s privacy policy.
