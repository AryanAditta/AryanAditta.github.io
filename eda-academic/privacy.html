Privacy & Analytics

Website analytics

This academic website uses Google Analytics in a privacy-preserving configuration. Analytics storage is denied by default, so the site does not place Google Analytics cookies unless you explicitly choose to allow them through “Analytics choices” in the footer.

With analytics storage denied, the Google tag can send limited cookieless measurement pings for aggregate page and event measurement. If you choose to allow Analytics cookies, reporting for sessions and returning visitors can become more accurate.

The site measures general activity such as page views, research and publications navigation, CV-link clicks and visits to professional profiles. Email campaign links contain general campaign labels only and never include recipient names, email addresses or professor-specific identifiers.

These statistics cannot reliably identify a particular professor or person. Ad blockers, browser privacy settings and cookieless measurement can reduce or model counts. CV clicks indicate that a link was opened, not proof that a document was read.

Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled. No email-open pixels, fingerprinting or recipient identifiers are used and this site does not separately log IP addresses.

Google processes analytics information according to How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Learn more in Google’s privacy policy.
