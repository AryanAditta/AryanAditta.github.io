In Preparation

Source-Aware Evaluation of RTL Area and Timing Prediction with Prospective Confirmation

Manuscript in preparation for submission to Integration, the VLSI Journal. The study uses source-aware grouped development data and model-specific prospective confirmation for mapped-area and pre-layout timing prediction under a Yosys/ABC/OpenSTA Nangate45 flow.

Fresh paired confirmations: Area MAE 8,382 → 3,015 µm² (58.9% lower primary loss); Timing MAE 0.167 → 0.136 ns (17.5% lower primary loss), with 39 paired designs in each primary cohort.
