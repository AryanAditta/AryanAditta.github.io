Research Experience

GNN-SABLE: GNN-Guided VLSI Floorplanning with Constraint-Aware Refinement

Independent Research · 2026–Present
  • Developing a learning-guided VLSI floorplanning framework that combines GNN-based placement guidance with B*-tree simulated annealing, legalization and exact-evaluated refinement.
  • Investigating trade-offs among wirelength, placement compactness and physical-design legality under boundary, grouping and related structural constraints.
  • Building a reproducible experimental workflow with controlled baselines, ablation studies, leakage-aware data handling, legality checks and stage-level evaluation for publication-quality benchmarking.

Leakage-Aware RTL-to-QoR Prediction for Early Area and Timing Estimation

Research Collaboration with Zakia Sultana, Ph.D. Candidate, MSCAD Laboratory, University of Arkansas · Ongoing
  • Developing machine-learning methods to predict mapped cell area and pre-layout timing directly from RTL before full implementation.
  • Working on source provenance, duplicate/near-duplicate control, leakage-aware grouped evaluation, RTL structural feature engineering and reproducible model-development pipelines.
  • Investigating size-aware prediction, model explainability, coverage/extrapolation analysis and out-of-distribution awareness for more reliable early-stage QoR estimation.

Open-Source RFIC Design and Verification of a 28-GHz Two-Stage Class-A SiGe Power Amplifier

Undergraduate Thesis, Department of ECE, KUET · Supervisor: Aysha Tabassum · 2026
  • Designed and simulated a two-stage 28-GHz Class-A SiGe power amplifier using the IHP SG13G2 130-nm SiGe BiCMOS Open PDK and an open-source RFIC workflow based on Qucs-S, ngspice/Xyce, KLayout and EM-aware modeling.
  • Developed a progressive ideal-passive → PDK-aware → EM-aware realization workflow to investigate the impact of physical passive implementation on gain, input/output matching, reverse isolation and stability.
  • Explored machine-learning-assisted design-space screening and optimization and extended the work toward layout/GDSII-level realization; a thesis-derived manuscript was submitted to ICCIT 2026.
