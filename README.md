## Current Research Identity

The portfolio reflects two complementary hardware-design directions:

1. **RF/mmWave integrated-circuit design**, where circuit simulation, process-aware realization, EM effects and physical implementation remain the primary engineering evidence.
2. **VLSI/EDA**, where learning is used to guide difficult design decisions while geometry, legality, provenance and physical evaluation remain explicit.

The broader research goal is to combine hardware understanding with computational methods without replacing engineering verification by machine-learning outputs.
