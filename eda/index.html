My EDA work focuses on the boundary between learned representations and explicit algorithmic reasoning: where machine learning can provide useful design guidance and where exact physical-design constraints still require structured optimization.

Physical Design Research · 2026–PresentFirst-author manuscript submitted

Learning-Guided Constraint-Rich VLSI Floorplanning

Investigating how graph-based learning can guide floorplanning under connectivity, geometric and placement constraints while keeping exact legality and physical-design requirements explicit.

  • The submitted GNN-SABLE work studies learned guidance for B*-tree-based floorplanning and evaluates how learning can improve the starting structure of a classical optimization process.
  • Ongoing experiments examine a broader neural-legality gap: models can learn useful global floorplan structure while exact local geometric feasibility remains substantially harder.
  • The current direction uses controlled encoder-versus-decoder studies to determine what should be learned and what should remain algorithmically enforced.

Research Collaboration · OngoingRTL-to-QoR

Leakage-Aware RTL-to-QoR Prediction for Early Area & Timing Estimation

Working with Zakia Sultana, Ph.D. Candidate at the MSCAD Laboratory, University of Arkansas, on early implementation-quality estimation directly from RTL before full technology mapping and static timing analysis.

  • Studies how structural information available at RTL can support early area and timing estimation.
  • Places strong emphasis on source provenance, duplicate-aware grouping and leakage-aware evaluation so reported performance reflects genuinely unseen source families.
  • Ongoing work explores structural-complexity effects, explainability and coverage warnings so predictions can be interpreted with appropriate engineering caution.
